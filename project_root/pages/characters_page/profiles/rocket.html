Origin

Rocket Raccoon first appeared in Marvel Preview #7 in 1976, created by Bill Mantlo and Keith Giffen. The character was a comparatively minor cosmic-Marvel figure for thirty years before the 2008 Annihilation Guardians of the Galaxy reformation and the 2014 James Gunn film. Like Star-Lord, Rocket's modern profile is film-driven.

Comic history

Most of Rocket's defining material is the Abnett-Lanning Guardians run and the Skottie Young 2014 solo series. The cinematic version is the cultural touchstone for most readers, and the Rivals design leans into the brown-and-white fur with the Bombard Mode jetpack as a callback to his comic identity as a Halfworld constable.

Rocket in Marvel Rivals

Rocket is a 250 HP Strategist whose kit is unusual for the role — he is the only Strategist whose primary identity is damage rather than healing. Bombard Mode is a high-DPS turret stance; Repair Mode is the healing toggle. The skill ceiling is in toggling between modes at the right moments. Players who lock into one mode underperform; players who flicker between them can both heal and contribute fight damage.

Abilities

Bombard Mode is the damage stance. Repair Mode is the healing stance. Jetpack Dash is the mobility. Flying Ace is the secondary mobility. C.Y.A., his ultimate, is a self-buff with high mobility and damage that lets him commit to a fight in a way Strategists typically cannot.
