Origin

Cloak and Dagger first appeared in The Spectacular Spider-Man #64 in March 1982, created by Bill Mantlo and Ed Hannigan. They were two runaway teenagers — Tyrone Johnson and Tandy Bowen — who had been experimented on with synthetic drugs and developed light-and-shadow powers as a result.

Comic history

Cloak and Dagger have always been a pair, which is unusual for Marvel character licensing. They have had two solo series in the 1980s, frequent X-Men crossovers, and a 2018 Freeform TV series. The Marvel Rivals design treats them as a single unit — the player swaps between Cloak's dark stance and Dagger's light stance dynamically.

Cloak and Dagger in Marvel Rivals

Cloak and Dagger is a 250 HP Strategist with the most unusual character switch mechanic in the role group. Cloak provides darkness-based mobility and disengage; Dagger provides light-based healing and damage. The kit rewards stance management — Dagger heals, Cloak escapes, and the swap window is the entire skill expression of the character.

Abilities

Darkforce Cloak is the Cloak-stance primary. Light's Embrace is the Dagger-stance heal. Terror Cape is the Cloak CC. Dark Teleportation is the disengage. Eternal Bond, the ultimate, links the two halves of the character together for a brief duration of empowered output.
