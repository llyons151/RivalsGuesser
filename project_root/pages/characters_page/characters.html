How we wrote these profiles

Every character profile on Rivaldle is hand-written by the team behind the site. We are not pulling from a wiki and we are not running a content farm. Each profile is intended to be useful to two distinct readers: a Marvel Rivals player who wants strategy notes on the hero, and a casual fan who wants comic context for the character. We do not paste in Wikipedia summaries. We do not generate the text. If a fact in a profile is wrong, we are the ones who got it wrong, and we want to hear about it so we can fix it.

Profiles are organized in five sections. Origin gives the character's first comic appearance and the creators behind it. Comic history covers the runs and arcs we consider most important to understanding who this character is on the page. In Marvel Rivals describes the role and feel of the character in the live game. Abilities walks through the kit. Spotting in Rivaldle is mode-by-mode advice for identifying that hero in the puzzle.
